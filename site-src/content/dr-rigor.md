## What it does

You choose a depth tier (quick, standard or deep) that sets how much it may spend. It plans sub-questions from several stakeholder perspectives, researches them, and then deliberately looks for evidence against its own conclusions. A separate verifier agent checks each tagged claim against the sources the run kept, and the result is a claim ledger. Gaps are stated in the report instead of smoothed over.

## Where it came from

dr-rigor is the verified half of a deep research skill that was split in two. It keeps the original five phases (brief, scan, deep dive, challenge and expansion, synthesis and verification), the tier budgets and search allowances, and the claim-ID and provenance rules. The verifier is deliberately independent of the writer, and its ledger statuses are carried over from the private library it was ported from. Two small scripts check citations and the ledger mechanically.
