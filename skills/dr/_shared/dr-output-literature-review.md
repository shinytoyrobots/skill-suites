# Form: literature review

Body sections, after the Executive Summary:

## Scope and Method
What literatures were searched, inclusion criteria, date range.

## What Is Established
Findings with strong, independent support. Each with evidence, confidence, and the studies or
sources behind it.

## What Is Contested
Where sources disagree: both sides, the evidence for each, and why they might differ (method,
population, date, incentive).

## Synthesis by Theme
The field's main lines of work, organized by theme, not by paper.

## Gaps in the Literature
What nobody has studied, as distinct from what this run couldn't find (which goes in Gaps and
Uncertainties). A claim that a gap exists in the literature is itself factual: tag it.

## Recommendations (optional)
Further reading or research, a numbered list, each ending `Basis: F{n}, ...`.
