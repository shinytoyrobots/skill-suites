# Form: strategic assessment

Body sections, after the Executive Summary:

## Key Findings
Grouped by theme. Each finding:
- **Finding**: what we learned
- **Evidence**: retained-source URLs
- **Confidence**: High / Medium / Low
- **Implication**: why it matters for the question

## Landscape / Context
Key players, trends, state of play.

## Recommendations
Actionable next steps, ranked by priority, as a numbered list. Each ends `Basis: F{n}, ...`.

## Next Steps
What to do in the next week or month, and what would change the recommendation.
