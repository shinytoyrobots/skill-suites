# Form: landscape map

Body sections, after the Executive Summary:

## Map
A table of the players: name, category, what they do, scale or traction signal, last activity
date, source.

## Segments
How the space divides, and who sits where.

## Trends and Direction
Where the space is heading, with evidence and confidence.

## White Space
What nobody is doing yet, and the evidence that the gap is real rather than unsearched.

## Key Findings
The few findings that matter most for the question, with evidence, confidence, and implication.

## Recommendations (optional)
If the brief asks for them: a numbered list, each ending `Basis: F{n}, ...`.
